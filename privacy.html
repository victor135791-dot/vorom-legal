Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

TL;DR: Your workout and Health data stays on your device (and optionally in your own private iCloud account if you turn on iCloud Backup). VOROM cannot access your iCloud backup and uses RevenueCat only for anonymous purchase analytics and active-installation counts. No ads, cross-app tracking, VOROM account, VOROM-server upload, or data resale.

What VOROM stores locally

VOROM stores the data you create inside the app — workout logs, exercise catalog edits, body weight, biological sex and birth year/month (used to calculate heart-rate zones and strength benchmarks), training-mode settings, plates, custom images, and app settings. This data is stored locally on your device. VOROM does not operate an app account or VOROM-hosted server-sync system.

Where your data lives & optional iCloud Backup

Your complete VOROM training database lives in the app's local SwiftData store on your device. iPhone ↔ Apple Watch workout sync uses Apple's WatchConnectivity framework between your own devices. VOROM does not upload your workouts, templates, exercises, heart-rate samples, GPS routes, distance traces, body weight, or app settings to VOROM-operated servers.

If you choose to enable iCloud Auto-Backup in Settings → Data (off by default) or trigger a manual backup, VOROM stores a compressed snapshot of your gym plans, folders, custom exercises, app preferences, native workout history, and manual nutrition logs in your personal Apple iCloud Private Database (CloudKit). This backup lives strictly inside your own Apple Account's private iCloud storage and is not accessible to VOROM. Apple Health–sourced data (such as imported Apple Fitness workouts, HealthKit heart-rate samples, HealthKit body composition/nutrition imports, and Recovery, Stress, and Strain scores) is excluded from iCloud Backup and recalculated from Apple Health on restore.

HealthKit

If you grant Health permission, VOROM reads heart rate, body weight, and (optionally) running power FROM Apple Health for use during workouts and in benchmarks. VOROM does NOT write workout data back to Apple Health unless you explicitly enable export. We do not use HealthKit data for marketing or advertising.

Apple Intelligence meal-photo analysis

If you choose to scan a meal photo, VOROM may use Apple's Foundation Models framework to estimate the food name, calories, and protein. Analysis runs on your device when an eligible on-device Apple Intelligence model is available. When VOROM uses Apple's Private Cloud Compute model for additional reasoning, the meal photo and the prompt information needed for that request are sent securely to Apple Private Cloud Compute solely to produce the requested estimate.

Apple states that data sent to and returned by Private Cloud Compute is not stored, is not made accessible to Apple, and is used only to fulfill the request. VOROM does not send meal photos to VOROM-operated servers, does not retain a server-side copy, and does not use meal photos for advertising, marketing, tracking, or model training. You control whether to initiate a scan. Generated nutrition values are approximate, remain editable, and are not saved until you review and confirm them.

Limited anonymous purchase analytics

VOROM uses RevenueCat to measure active app installations and analyze purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app identifier, purchase history, last app activity, and basic app/platform information. The identifier is not connected to a VOROM account, name, or email address.

VOROM does not send RevenueCat your workout data, HealthKit data, body weight, GPS routes, distance traces, notes, photos, contacts, calendar, or advertising identifiers. We do not sell or rent data, and we do not use this information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

App Store privacy label

For Apple's App Privacy label, VOROM discloses Purchase History for Analytics and App Functionality. Because RevenueCat uses an anonymous identifier that is not connected to a VOROM account or contact information, the information is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking. Optional iCloud Backup writes only to your own private Apple iCloud container, which VOROM cannot access. Workout, HealthKit, location, body-weight, and other training data is never collected by VOROM.

Third-party services (sub-processors)

VOROM does not use advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking.

  • RevenueCat — receives anonymous purchase history and app-activity information to provide purchase analytics, active-user statistics, and receipt validation. VOROM does not provide RevenueCat with workout, HealthKit, location, body-weight, name, email, or advertising-identifier data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — handles purchases, Lifetime licenses, subscriptions, cancellations, refunds, and purchase restoration. VOROM reads StoreKit entitlement status only to unlock paid features; we do not receive your payment card details.
  • Apple iCloud / CloudKit (Private Database) — if you opt in to iCloud Backup in Settings → Data, Apple stores your encrypted backup snapshot inside your own private iCloud account under Apple's iCloud privacy protections. VOROM cannot view, query, or access your private iCloud backup.
  • Apple Health / HealthKit — if you grant permission, VOROM reads selected health and workout-related data from Apple Health for app features. You can change this permission in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Apple WatchConnectivity — transfers workout state between your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Apple Intelligence / Foundation Models / Private Cloud Compute — when you initiate meal-photo analysis on a supported device, Apple Intelligence processes the photo on-device or through Private Cloud Compute to return the requested estimate. Private Cloud Compute request content is not retained or made accessible to Apple. VOROM does not receive or retain a server-side copy.

International data transfer

VOROM does not transfer your training database to VOROM-operated servers. RevenueCat may process anonymous purchase and app-activity information in countries where it operates. When you initiate a meal-photo scan that uses Private Cloud Compute, Apple may process the request on Apple silicon servers under its Private Cloud Compute privacy protections. Apple may also process App Store, StoreKit, HealthKit, and Apple Watch-related data according to Apple's own policies and your device settings.

Data retention

Training data remains on your device until you delete it, wipe the app's local data, or uninstall the app. VOROM does not keep a server-side training database for restore. RevenueCat retains anonymous purchase and app-activity records according to its privacy and retention policies. Apple may retain App Store purchase records according to Apple's terms and policies.

Deleting your data

Because there is no VOROM account or server-stored training database, there is no server account to delete. You can delete workouts, plans, exercises, body-weight entries, and other local records inside the app. Deleting the app from your device removes the app's local data unless you previously exported it or your device backup preserves it. To request deletion of an anonymous RevenueCat customer record, contact support@vorom.app.

Offline template sharing

When you share a template via VOROM's Offline Template Share feature:

  • The template content (exercises, sets, notes, etc.) is encoded directly into a private deep link URL.
  • VOROM does not receive, store, log, or transmit the template content. The data resides entirely within the URL string itself.
  • The link is sent through your own messaging app of choice (iMessage, WhatsApp, AirDrop, email, etc.) to recipient(s) you choose.
  • Only the people you send the link to can decode and import the template.
  • VOROM has no record, log, or copy of shares performed via this feature.

Template reports and content blocks

VOROM does not send template reports or template content to a VOROM backend. If you receive an objectionable or infringing template link from someone else, contact the sender or the messaging service used to send it. You can also email support@vorom.app with concerns, but VOROM may not be able to review or block content that exists only inside a private link we never received.

Export

Settings → Data → Export My Data generates a JSON + CSV snapshot of your training data. The export always returns your full unrestricted history regardless of tier. Because VOROM is on-device, we strongly recommend exporting periodically and saving the file somewhere outside the app, such as iCloud Drive, Files, AirDrop, or email.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA / similar laws)

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California, or any jurisdiction with similar data-protection laws, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or erase personal data. VOROM's Export My Data feature gives you a copy of the local data stored by the app. Because VOROM does not maintain an account or server database for your training history, most access and deletion actions happen directly on your device. For questions, contact support@vorom.app.

Children

VOROM is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom).

Changes

If this policy changes, the app will show a banner on next launch with a summary and link to the new version. Continued use means acceptance.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support@vorom.app.
